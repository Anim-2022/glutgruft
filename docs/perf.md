# Performance report

Generated by `npx vite-node scripts/perf.ts 3` on 2026-11-25.
Machine: Intel(R) Xeon(R) Processor @ 2.10GHz × 4, headless Chromium (software rendering).

Scene: cave floor 7, 12 awake monsters on screen, a burning oil field (≈ 40 fire tiles), barrels, one turn every 250 ms.
Each configuration: 3 runs, 2 s warm-up excluded, 4 s sampled; values are medians over the runs.
The game renders at 640×360 internally and is scaled, so the viewport mainly affects the final blit.

Budget at 60 FPS is 16.7 ms per frame. Effect arrays are capped (particles 400, splatter 160, smoke 160, numbers 60),
so long fights do not accumulate state; everything resets on a new floor (`resetView`).

## Optimisation in this pass

Profiling the bench (Chrome CPU profile) showed `drawImage` as the top cost: the heat shimmer above every strong
fire tile copied the frame onto itself. It now runs only on *strong* effects and for at most 8 tiles per frame.

| Viewport | Effects | drawRun median before (ms) | after (ms) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1280×720 | normal | 14.70 | 10.60 |
| 1280×720 | strong | 15.30 | 12.20 |
| 1920×1080 | normal | 14.00 | 10.00 |
| 1920×1080 | strong | 15.50 | 12.60 |

Next largest costs are the light pass (`addLight`, `applyLight`, about 35 % of the frame in this scene).
In normal play (fewer burning tiles) frames stay well below the 16.7 ms budget.
